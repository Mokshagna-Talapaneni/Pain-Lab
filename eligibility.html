What Participation Involves

What Participation Involves

As a study participant, you will complete a series of guided tDCS sessions over an 11-day period. Most sessions are conducted from home via Zoom with a member of our research team. The baseline visit takes place at our lab on the University of Arizona campus.

Study Schedule

  • Day 1: Baseline data collection at our lab (in-person)
  • Days 2–5: Home-based intervention sessions via Zoom
  • Day 6: Week 2 data collection + intervention session
  • Days 7–10: Home-based intervention sessions via Zoom
  • Day 11: Final data collection + intervention session

Compensation

Eligible participants may receive up to $270 in compensation for their time and contribution to the study. Compensation may be provided in stages as you complete each phase of the study.
