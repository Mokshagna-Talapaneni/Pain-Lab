Self-Administered Neuromodulation Program

Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS)

This study investigates the efficacy of home-based transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) for chronic pain management, guided remotely via Zoom sessions with our research team. Participants will receive a tDCS device and be guided through self-administered sessions over a structured two-week program.

Enroll in This Study
Study Image

Non-Invasive

Safe, gentle brain stimulation with no surgical procedures required.

Home-Based via Zoom

Participate from the comfort of your home with guided virtual sessions.

Two-Week Program

A structured 11-day study designed to fit into your schedule.
