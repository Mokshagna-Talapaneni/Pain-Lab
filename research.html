Currently Active

Study 1: Dual-Site tDCS for Knee OA with Chronic Widespread Pain

Who & Why

  • Adults 50–85 with knee OA + chronic widespread pain (pain in ≥4 of 5 body regions for >3 months)
  • A neglected, high-burden subgroup: more pain, more catastrophizing, anxiety and depression
  • M1 for the sensory side + L-DLPFC for the cognitive-emotional side

Design (USASP-Funded Pilot)

Screening & baseline visit
Randomize 1:1 (double-blind)
Active vs. sham dual-site tDCS — 10 sessions · Mon–Fri × 2 weeks
Post-treatment + monthly follow-up × 3

2 mA · 20 min M1 + 20 min F3 · order counterbalanced · home-based, Zoom-supervised · n = 25/group

Outcomes Now

Pain (NRS), KOOS, pain catastrophizing (PCS), pain anxiety (PASS-20), depression (PROMIS), QST

Non-Invasive

Safe, gentle brain stimulation with no surgical procedures required.

Home-Based via Zoom

Participate from the comfort of your home with guided virtual sessions.

Two-Week Program

A structured 11-day study designed to fit into your schedule.

Up to $270 Compensation

Receive compensation for your valuable time and participation.

Currently Active

Study 2: RESHAPE-TKA, tDCS + Exercise After Knee Replacement

The Problem

  • 15–34% have persistent pain after total knee arthroplasty (TKA)
  • Driven partly by central sensitization
  • Less M1 drive to the thigh muscles ("voluntary activation deficit") → weakness
  • Exercise alone often isn't enough

M1-tDCS Does Two Jobs

1 · Pain Brake

Engages descending pain modulation to calm central sensitization

2 · Priming

Raises M1 excitability so the exercise that follows produces more muscle activation and strength gain

Analogy: tDCS "tills the soil," exercise "plants the seeds."

Design

  • Triple-blind, sham-controlled RCT (OACTN Stage A)
  • Active vs. sham M1-tDCS + PT-directed lower-body strengthening
  • 2 mA · 20 min · 2×/week · 8 weeks; exercise right after tDCS
  • Home-based, tele-supervised
  • Co-primary: KOOS Pain & ADL

PI: C. Kent Kwoh, MD · MPIs: Chiyoung Lee, Daniel K. White
