Who We Are

About Our Lab

The Pain Neuroscience Research Lab at the University of Arizona College of Nursing is dedicated to understanding the neural mechanisms underlying chronic pain and developing novel, non-pharmacological interventions. Our research focuses on translating neuroscience discoveries into accessible treatments for individuals living with pain.

We are committed to advancing evidence-based neuromodulation therapies, including transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS), to improve the quality of life for individuals with chronic pain conditions.

Self-Administered Neuromodulation Program

Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS)

This study investigates the efficacy of home-based transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) for chronic pain management, guided remotely via Zoom sessions with our research team. Participants may receive up to $270 in compensation.
